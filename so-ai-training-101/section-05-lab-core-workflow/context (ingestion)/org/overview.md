# Mary's Place — Organization Overview

## About

- Nonprofit operating family shelters and housing-stability programs in Seattle / King County
- Core focus: ensuring no child sleeps outside

## Mission

- Ensure no child sleeps outside by centering equity and opportunity for women and families
- Deliver a continuum of services: emergency shelter, mobile outreach, diversion, and housing supports
- Help families regain stable housing

## Vision

- A community where all families have safety, stability, and housing
- Guides shelter, prevention, and rapid re-housing efforts across King County

## Core Values

- **Love** — Welcome, respect, accept, and care for every guest
- **Collaboration** — Partner regionally to expand services and elevate family voices
- **Equity** — Ensure inclusive environments and equitable access for all
- **Stewardship** — Carefully manage financial, material, human, and story-based resources
- **Accountability** — Hold board, staff, volunteers, and guests to commitments
- **Responsiveness** — Address urgent needs with creativity, adaptability, and persistence

## Organizational Goals

- Move families quickly into safe, stable housing
- Expand access and operational efficiency across shelters and programs
- Reduce manual processes (e.g., bed-availability tracking)
- Improve real-time resource visibility
- Maximize social impact
- Prototype scalable solutions that can extend beyond a single agency
