# Mary's Place — Operational Challenges

## Key Opportunities

- Streamlined intake system to help families prioritize and navigate their path to housing security
- Inventory management system that tracks real-time family needs, enables targeted corporate donations, and facilitates efficient redistribution

---

## Shelter Services

- Length of stay increasing due to housing affordability and economic pressures in King County
- Manual intake process requiring multiple separate team assessments (health, housing, youth services)
- No centralized journey mapping — unable to estimate timelines or sequence complex steps for families
- Outdated database system (1990s-era SQL) requiring significant customization
- Double data entry — information manually entered into both the internal system and county-wide HMIS
- No integration between internal database and county HMIS

## Mobile Outreach Services

- Cannot effectively supply household items to outreach families
- Ad hoc ordering — outreach specialists call the warehouse with no visibility into inventory
- Hit-or-miss fulfillment — families receive whatever is available, not what they need
- Short lead times (often only days) when families move into housing, making sourcing difficult

## Prevention Services

- No established system for distributing goods to prevention families
- Prevention families lack marketplace access available to shelter guests

## Goods Management & Distribution (Cross-Cutting)

- Only 15–20% of donated items are usable by Mary's Place families
- No inventory management system — tracking relies on manual bin counts, not SKU-level detail
- Unable to target corporate donations due to lack of real-time needs data
- Excess unusable inventory accumulates with no redistribution system to other organizations
- Limited storage capacity at outreach specialist offices
- Inefficient information flow between supply (warehouse) and demand (families across all service areas)
- No centralized demand tracking across shelter, outreach, and prevention populations
